# Retail-Pricing-Analysis
Retail Price Optimization: Analyzing Demand, Competitor Pricing, and Revenue Opportunities
This project explores how an online retailer can use historical sales data, product pricing, and competitor information to identify opportunities for improving its pricing strategy.

**Business Problem**
An online retailer manages pricing across multiple product categories, but product-level pricing decisions may be made without a clear understanding of how historical prices relate to customer demand. 
This creates uncertainty about which products might benefit from a pricing review and when competitor positioning or seasonal factors should inform decisions.

This project examines historical sales, selling prices, and competitor price benchmarks to identify demand patterns and potential opportunities to improve sales performance and competitiveness.


**Business Question**
How do product prices relate to customer demand, and where can a retailer adjust its pricing strategy to improve sales performance and competitiveness?

Supporting questions

1.Where does revenue come from across products, categories, and time?
    Identifies the products and categories where pricing decisions have the greatest financial impact.
    
2.How does sales volume change when a product is priced above or below its historical average?
    Helps identify possible price sensitivity and evaluate pricing opportunities.
    
3.How do competitor prices and seasonal patterns relate to sales performance?
    Helps determine when competitive pricing or seasonal promotions may be worth considering.
