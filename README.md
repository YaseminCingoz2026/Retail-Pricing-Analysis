# Retail-Pricing-Analysis
Retail Price Optimization: Analyzing Demand, Competitor Pricing, and Revenue Opportunities
This project explores how an online retailer can use historical sales data, product pricing, and competitor information to identify opportunities for improving its pricing strategy.

**Business Problem**
An online retailer manages pricing across multiple product categories, but pricing decisions are often made at the individual product level without a clear understanding of how historical price changes relate to customer demand.

**Business Question**
How do product prices relate to customer demand, and where can a retailer adjust its pricing strategy to improve sales performance and competitiveness?
